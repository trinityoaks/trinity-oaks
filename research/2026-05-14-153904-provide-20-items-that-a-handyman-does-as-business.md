● Travel Charges — Industry Standard

  Most handymen do not include travel for free. Here's how it typically breaks
  down:

  Common Approaches

  ┌────────────────┬────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┐
  │     Method     │     Typical Charge     │          When to Use          │
  ├────────────────┼────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────┤
  │ Free within    │ First 10–15 miles free │ Best for marketing; absorb    │
  │ local radius   │                        │ cost into hourly rate         │
  ├────────────────┼────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────┤
  │ Flat           │                        │ Simple, predictable for       │
  │ trip/service   │ $25–$75 per visit      │ customer                      │
  │ fee            │                        │                               │
  ├────────────────┼────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────┤
  │ Per-mile       │ $0.70–$1.50/mile       │ Fair for occasional far jobs  │
  │ beyond radius  │ (round trip)           │                               │
  ├────────────────┼────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────┤
  │ Minimum        │ $75–$150 (covers 1st   │ Industry standard — protects  │
  │ service call   │ hour + travel)         │ against tiny jobs             │
  ├────────────────┼────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────┤
  │ Billable       │ Half the hourly rate,  │ For long-distance/commercial  │
  │ travel time    │ or full rate after 30  │ work                          │
  │                │ min                    │                               │
  └────────────────┴────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┘

  Recommended Structure for a Solo Handyman

  1. 0–15 miles: Included (baked into hourly rate)
  2. 15–30 miles: $35–$50 flat trip fee
  3. 30+ miles: $1.00/mile round trip OR bill drive time at $35–$45/hr (half
  rate)
  4. Emergency / same-day: Add $50–$100 surcharge
  5. Always enforce a minimum: $95–$125 minimum, even for a 10-minute job

  Rules of Thumb

  - IRS mileage rate for 2026: ~$0.70/mile — that's your cost floor, not what
  you charge. Charge 1.5–2× that to cover wear, time, and gas.
  - Disclose travel fees upfront on your quote — surprise travel charges are the
   #1 complaint in handyman reviews.
  - Bundle visits in the same area on the same day to avoid eating travel costs.
  - For repeat customers within your service zone, keep it free — loyalty is
  worth more than $25.

  Bottom line: Include travel within ~15 miles, charge $35–$50 flat beyond that,
   and never leave the driveway for less than your $95+ minimum.

  - Material markup: 25–50% on paint and supplies.
  - Bond + insurance required on most commercial jobs >$10K (factor 2–4% of

  - Net 30 payment terms are standard — price in 1.5–2% for cash flow cost.
