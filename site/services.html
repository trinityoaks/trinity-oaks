Full-Service Construction

From Foundation to Finish

One contractor, one point of contact, every trade coordinated. Below is what we do — if you don't see it here, ask us.

Custom Home Building

Design-build or build-to-plans. We handle permitting, sitework, framing, finishes, and every trade in between.

  • New construction on your lot or ours
  • Single-family and luxury custom homes
  • Architect and engineer coordination
  • Energy-efficient framing and insulation

Home Additions

Add square footage without giving up the home you already love. We tie new construction seamlessly into existing structures.

  • Master suites and bedroom additions
  • Sunrooms, family rooms, and bonus rooms
  • Second-story add-ons
  • Detached garages and accessory buildings

Kitchen Remodeling

From cabinet refresh to gut renovation. We open walls, move utilities, and finish with the cabinetry, counters, and lighting you've been picturing.

  • Custom and semi-custom cabinetry
  • Quartz, granite, and butcher-block countertops
  • Tile and stone backsplashes
  • Layout reconfiguration and island builds

Bathroom Remodeling

Whether it's a hall bath refresh or a full primary suite spa, we handle plumbing, electrical, tile, and finish work in-house.

  • Walk-in showers and freestanding tubs
  • Custom tile and stone work
  • Vanities, fixtures, and lighting
  • Aging-in-place accessibility upgrades

Decks & Outdoor Living

Extend your living space outside with weather-tough materials and finishes that look as good in year ten as they do in year one.

  • Composite and pressure-treated decks
  • Screened porches and pergolas
  • Outdoor kitchens and fire features
  • Hardscape patios and walkways

Whole-Home Renovations

Bringing an older home up to modern standards — or making a new house your own. We phase the work to match your budget and life.

  • Open-concept conversions
  • Basement finishing
  • Historic renovation and restoration
  • Energy and systems upgrades

Light Commercial

Tenant build-outs, office renovations, and small commercial new construction across Winston-Salem.

  • Office and retail tenant improvements
  • Restaurant and storefront build-outs
  • ADA-compliance upgrades
  • Property maintenance and turnkey services

Repairs & Handyman Services

For our existing clients, smaller jobs handled by the same team you already trust. No mystery subs.

  • Drywall, paint, and trim repairs
  • Door and window replacement
  • Storm damage and insurance work
  • Punch list and warranty service
